# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and constructive in all interactions
- Welcome contributors of all experience levels
- Focus feedback on code and ideas, not people
- Accept that maintainers have final say on direction

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks or harassment of any kind
- Dismissing contributions without explanation
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported by opening a private issue or emailing the maintainer directly.
Maintainers reserve the right to remove comments or ban users who violate these standards.
